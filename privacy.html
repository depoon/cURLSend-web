Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for cURL Send.

Effective date: May 17, 2026

Overview

cURL Send is an iOS developer tool for importing cURL commands, sending API requests, inspecting responses, saving requests, and storing reusable response values. The app does not require an account and is designed around local storage on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

cURL Send may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Saved requests you create.
  • Recent request history.
  • Response field bindings and Global Container values you save.
  • Onboarding completion state.

Network Requests

When you send a request, cURL Send sends that request from your device to the endpoint specified in your cURL command or edited request fields. The destination server is chosen by you. Any data included in the request, such as URLs, headers, request bodies, tokens, or credentials, is sent to that destination as part of the request you initiate.

Clipboard

The app includes clipboard actions for importing cURL text and copying request or response content. Clipboard access happens when you use app features that paste or copy content.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

cURL Send does not use advertising, does not require user tracking, and does not create a user account. No analytics behavior was included in the app behavior used to prepare this policy.

Data Sharing

cURL Send does not sell your data. Data leaves the app only when you choose to send a request to an endpoint, copy information, or otherwise use iOS features that move information outside the app.
